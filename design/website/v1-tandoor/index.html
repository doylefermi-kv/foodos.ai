FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What happens when the internet goes down?

Billing, KOTs and printing keep working on the device. Everything queues locally and syncs the moment you're back online — you'll never stop a queue because the router did.

Can you move my existing menu and data across?

Yes. Share an export or even a printed menu and we'll set up categories, items, variants, taxes and recipes for you before go-live. Scope TBD

Do I need new hardware?

No. FoodOS runs on the Android tablets and Windows machines most restaurants already have, with standard thermal printers and cash drawers. Exact hardware matrix TBD

Are the bills GST-compliant?

Yes — CGST/SGST or IGST split by item tax rate, HSN codes, and a monthly GST summary your CA can file from.

Is my sales data used to train the AI?

Policy to be set by CTO. Our intended stance: your data trains models for your outlets only, never pooled with other restaurants without explicit opt-in.

How long does setup take?

Typically one day for a single outlet, including menu entry, printer setup and a staff training session. To confirm
