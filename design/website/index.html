FoodOS.ai marketing website — 3 directions, one selected

Three complete homepage concepts for the pre-launch FoodOS.ai site. Each is a self-contained HTML file with no external fonts or CDNs, fully responsive, and uses realistic Indian restaurant data throughout. Direction C was selected and has been updated to match the CTO's decisions; A and B are unchanged below as a record of the comparison. Anything still unconfirmed — customer logos, stats, testimonials, the language translations — stays visibly marked as a placeholder on the page itself.

Direction C

✓ Selected by the CTO

Bazaar — bold Indian-local

High-contrast and confident. Ink on paper-white with marigold, indigo and pomegranate blocks, hard 2px rules and offset shadows, chunky display type. The POS mock is drawn as an actual thermal receipt with GSTIN and HSN on it. Indian-ness comes from the operational detail — ₹, GST, UPI, Hinglish in the AI demos — and from colour blocking, not from ornament. Updated post-decision: four confirmed AI capabilities including a regional-language switcher with a scroll-wheel animation across nine Indian languages, no pricing section, and a real logo mark.

Direction A

Not selected

Tandoor — warm & appetising

Food-led and inviting. Cream canvas, editorial serif headlines, saffron and chilli accents, generous spacing and soft shadows. Reads as hospitality rather than software — closest in feel to a restaurant brand, which makes it approachable for owners who find POS software intimidating. The AI section inverts to a dark warm panel so it still lands as the differentiator.

Decisions made (CTO, 13 Oct 2026)

  • Direction C — “Bazaar” is the chosen direction.
  • AI capabilities: smart inventory & auto-reorder, menu & pricing insights, WhatsApp / voice ordering, plus regional language support across nine Indian languages. Demand forecasting and “ask your sales” are dropped.
  • Framing: present tense — the features are described as working today.
  • CTA: “Book a demo”, everywhere.
  • Pricing section removed for now.
  • AI data-privacy stance is off the public FAQ until the architecture is settled.
  • Brand: “FoodOS.ai”, and a real logo is approved.

Still open

  • The nine language translations are placeholders and need native-speaker review before anything ships. They're marked as such on the page.
  • The logo in C is my proposal, built in SVG/CSS. If it's going to a designer or agency, it needs a brief — that's a CTO call, not something I should start.
  • Form handling — nothing submits. Demo requests need a destination.
  • Remaining placeholders: customer logos, stats, testimonials, hardware list, setup time, entity name.
  • Pages beyond the homepage, if this becomes a full site rather than a landing page.
