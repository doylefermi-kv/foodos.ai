FAQ

Straight answers.

What happens when the internet drops?

Billing, KOT and printing are local-first — they keep running on the device. Everything queues and syncs when the connection returns.

Where does the AI run, and is my data used to train it?

Model hosting & data policy to be decided by CTO. Proposed stance: per-outlet models trained on your data only, never pooled across restaurants without explicit opt-in, with every AI answer traceable to source bills.

Can I migrate from Petpooja or another POS?

Yes — menu, categories, variants, taxes, recipes and customer lists. We do the import and verify it with you before go-live. Scope TBD

Do I need special hardware?

No. Android tablets or Windows billing machines you already own, plus standard thermal printers, cash drawers and scanners. Hardware matrix TBD

Are bills GST-compliant?

Item-level tax rates with CGST/SGST or IGST split, HSN codes, and monthly summaries ready for filing.

How fast is onboarding?

One day for a single outlet including menu setup, printer config and staff training; a phased rollout for chains. To confirm
