FAQ

Seedha jawab.

Internet band ho gaya toh?

Billing, KOT and printing keep running on the device itself. Everything syncs the moment the connection is back — you never stop a queue because the router did.

Will you move my existing menu and data?

Yes — categories, items, variants, taxes, recipes and customer lists, from Petpooja or any other POS. We do the import and check it with you before go-live. Scope TBD

Do I need to buy new hardware?

No. It runs on the Android tablets and Windows billing machines most restaurants already have, with standard thermal printers and cash drawers. Hardware list TBD

Is the bill properly GST-compliant?

Item-level tax rates with CGST/SGST or IGST, HSN codes, your GSTIN on the invoice, and a monthly summary your CA can file from.

Kya yeh meri bhasha mein chalega?

Yes. Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati and English — across the billing screen, the printed KOT and the WhatsApp replies your customers get. Each staff member picks their own, so the kitchen and the counter don't have to agree. Final language list TBD

Kitna time lagta hai setup mein?

Usually one day for a single outlet — menu entry, printer setup and a training session with your staff. To confirm
