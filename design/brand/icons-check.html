FOO-5 · exported assets

The files, at the size they ship

Every image below is the real exported file, not a live SVG. Magnified views use nearest-neighbour so you can count pixels. This page also sets the tab icon from favicon.svg / favicon.ico — check the browser tab.

1 — favicon, at 1:1

The same drawing as the app icons — there is no separate small cut. Each image below is rendered at its own size rather than downscaled from a bigger one, so this is the real 16px, not a smooth resample of the 32.

2 — magnified 8×

The real pixel grids. 16px is the floor the geometry was tuned against: the bars are 13 units deep, which lands as just over 2px, and the torn edge still breaks the silhouette rather than reading as a plain rectangle.

3 — App icons, at 1:1 and shaped

The mark, full bleed. iOS and Android round the corners themselves, so these ship square. The dashed circle on the maskable icon is Android's 80% safe zone.

4 — Maskable icon vs the safe circle

Android crops this to whatever the launcher wants. The chit's corners sit at a radius of 197.5 inside the guaranteed 204.8 circle, so a circle mask, a squircle mask and a full square all keep the whole ticket — including the torn edge, which is the first thing a tight crop would eat.

On dark browser chrome the ink tile sinks into the UI. favicon.svg handles that with a prefers-color-scheme: dark rule that adds the marigold keyline; the .ico cannot carry a media query, so Safari — which ignores SVG favicons — gets the plain ink tile. That is the one place the mark is quieter than it should be, and it is a browser limit rather than a drawing problem.
